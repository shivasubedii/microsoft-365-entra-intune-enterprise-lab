# Employee Offboarding Workflow

## Objective
Remove access promptly while preserving organizational data according to business requirements.

## Workflow
1. Confirm authorized termination/offboarding request.
2. Block/disable user sign-in.
3. Revoke active sessions.
4. Reset credentials where required.
5. Remove unnecessary group memberships and privileged roles.
6. Address mailbox ownership/delegation requirements.
7. Preserve/transfer organizational files according to policy.
8. Remove or reassign licenses at the appropriate stage.
9. Retire or remove managed-device access where applicable.
10. Verify access is no longer possible.
11. Document actions and completion.

## Security Principle
Offboarding should be repeatable, auditable and verified rather than treated as a single “disable account” action.
