# Employee Onboarding Workflow

## Scenario
A new Finance employee requires Microsoft 365 access.

## Workflow
1. Confirm approved employee information.
2. Create the Entra ID user.
3. Set department and job attributes.
4. Add the employee to approved security groups.
5. Assign the required Microsoft 365 license.
6. Require secure authentication/MFA registration.
7. Provision Exchange Online and collaboration access.
8. Assign SharePoint/Teams resources according to role.
9. Enroll the corporate endpoint where device management is in scope.
10. Validate sign-in, mailbox and authorized resources.
11. Record completion.

## Verification
A successful onboarding is not complete merely because the account exists. Confirm that the employee can access required services and cannot access restricted departmental resources.
