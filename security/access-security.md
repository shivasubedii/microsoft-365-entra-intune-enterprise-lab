# MFA and Conditional Access

## MFA
Multi-factor authentication reduces reliance on passwords alone. Administrative troubleshooting should distinguish between credential failure, MFA registration failure, authentication-method issues and policy-based blocking.

## Conditional Access Portfolio Design
Example scenarios:
- Require MFA for selected cloud applications.
- Require stronger controls for administrative roles.
- Block or restrict access that does not meet organizational policy.
- Require a compliant device for sensitive resources where licensing and organizational policy support it.

## Important
Conditional Access configurations documented here are portfolio designs. They should be tested carefully in a real tenant with emergency-access planning to avoid administrator lockout.

## Verification
Use sign-in information and policy results to determine why access was granted or denied rather than weakening security controls without diagnosis.
