# Scenario: Intune Device Noncompliant
**Problem:** A managed-device scenario blocks access to protected resources.

**Investigation:** Check enrollment, device record, last check-in, assigned compliance policies and failed compliance settings.

**Root Cause:** Device does not meet a required compliance condition or has not reported current status.

**Resolution:** Remediate the failed requirement and synchronize/re-evaluate the device.

**Verification:** Device reports compliant and authorized access succeeds.

**Scope:** Portfolio design scenario.
