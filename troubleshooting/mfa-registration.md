# Scenario: MFA Registration Failure
**Problem:** A user cannot complete secure sign-in.

**Symptoms:** Password is accepted but authentication-method registration or MFA challenge fails.

**Investigation:** Verify account status, authentication methods, registration requirement and relevant sign-in/policy information.

**Likely Root Cause:** Authentication method is missing, incorrectly registered or unavailable.

**Resolution:** Follow approved identity-verification and authentication-method recovery procedures.

**Verification:** User completes MFA and accesses authorized services.

**Prevention:** Provide onboarding instructions and maintain approved recovery procedures.
