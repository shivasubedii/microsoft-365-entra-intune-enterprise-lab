# Scenario: Missing Microsoft 365 License
**Problem:** A newly created employee cannot use an expected Microsoft 365 service.

**Investigation:** Confirm account status, assigned licenses, enabled service plans and group-based assignment where applicable.

**Root Cause:** Required service/license was not assigned or provisioned.

**Resolution:** Apply the approved license/service assignment.

**Verification:** Confirm provisioning and successful service access.

**Prevention:** Add license verification to the onboarding checklist.
